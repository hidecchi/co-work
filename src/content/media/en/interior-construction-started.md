Interior construction has started at **ONE COIN COWORKING**, a 7-minute walk from [Motomachi Station](/en/#information) in Kobe.

We are preparing the space for its scheduled opening at the end of October, including partition walls, electrical wiring, and other fit-out work.

## Construction progress

![Overview of the interior work, including flooring and ceiling equipment installation](/column/interior-construction-started/overview.jpg)

Work is progressing across the space, including flooring and ceiling equipment installation.

![Partition wall framing and electrical wiring](/column/interior-construction-started/partition-framing.jpg)

Partition framing and wiring are also moving forward, and the layout of the coworking area is gradually taking shape.

## What comes next

Once interior work is complete, we will install furniture, carry out final cleaning and inspections, and prepare for opening.

For more about our opening plans, see our [opening announcement](/en/column/motomachi-kakuyasu-coworking/). For media inquiries and other questions, please use our [contact form](/en/contact/).
