Fiber installation at **ONE COIN COWORKING**, a 7-minute walk from [Motomachi Station](/en/#information) in Kobe, is complete. We’re now live on the **Cross 10 Giga** plan.

Ahead of opening, we’re running Wi-Fi speed checks inside the space.

## Current speed check

At the time of measurement, download speed came in at about 370 Mbps.

![Fast.com Wi-Fi speed test result of about 370 Mbps](/column/cross-10giga-opened/speed-test.jpg)

*Figures vary by measurement conditions and timing.*

The line itself is a 10 Giga plan, but actual Wi-Fi speeds will go up or down depending on the device and how far you are from the router (seat location). We’ll keep checking connectivity around the floor.

## What’s next

Alongside final interior finishing, we’re fine-tuning power and Wi-Fi so everything is ready for guests.

For more about our opening plans, see our [opening announcement](/en/column/motomachi-kakuyasu-coworking/). For media inquiries and other questions, please use our [contact form](/en/contact/).
