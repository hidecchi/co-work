A budget coworking space is opening near Motomachi Station in Kobe: **ONE COIN COWORKING**. We plan to open in late October. Entry is ¥500 for unlimited stay, with no membership required. After payment, you can start working, studying, or taking a break right away.

If you have been searching for affordable coworking in Kobe or Motomachi, this is a simple drop-in option close to the station.

## Affordable coworking in Kobe

Many coworking spaces in Kobe use monthly memberships or hourly rates. Even a drop-in visit can cost around ¥1,000, so short sessions and long focus days both add up.

ONE COIN COWORKING charges **¥500 entry for unlimited time**. There is no extra fee whether you stay one hour or all day. It is built for freelancers, remote workers, and students who want cheap coworking in Kobe without a contract.

## 7 minutes on foot from Motomachi Station

The space is at [4-10-31 Nakayamate-dori, Chuo-ku, Kobe, Hyogo 650-0004](/en/#information), a 7-minute walk from Motomachi Station. From the station, walk north along Koikawa-suji; we are next to Co-op Yamate.

Sannomiya and Motomachi are well connected by train and bus, including trips from Osaka. For anyone looking for cheap coworking in Motomachi, being within walking distance of the station is a real advantage.
