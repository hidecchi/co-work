**ONE COIN COWORKING** will open in late October, a 7-minute walk from Motomachi Station in Kobe.

## Pricing

Many coworking spaces in Kobe operate on monthly memberships or hourly rates, and even a short visit can cost around ¥1,000.

We offer **¥500 entry for unlimited time**, at the same rate whether you stay one hour or all day.

## Location

[4-10-31 Nakayamate-dori, Chuo-ku, Kobe, Hyogo](/en/#information) (7-minute walk from Motomachi Station)

From Motomachi Station, walk north along Koikawa-suji. We are located next to Co-op Yamate.

- Address: 4-10-31 Nakayamate-dori, Chuo-ku, Kobe, Hyogo 650-0004, Japan
- Access: 7-minute walk from Motomachi Station
- Hours: 24 hours

## How We Operate

Our unmanned, no-membership model helps us keep prices low. After entering, simply pay at the tablet on your left to begin using the space immediately.

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Price | ¥500 entry (unlimited time) |
| Membership | Not required |
| Payment | Cashless only |
| Hours | 24 hours |
| Seats | 20–30 (planned) |

## Amenities

- Wireless LAN (Wi-Fi)
- Power outlets
- Counter seats and table seats
- Outside food and drinks permitted
- Conversation permitted

## How to Use

1. After entering, pay the ¥500 entry fee on the tablet to your left
2. Take any available seat for work, study, or rest
3. Before leaving, please wipe the table if it is dirty

As the space is unmanned, we ask for your cooperation with light cleaning before you leave. For details, see [How to Use](/en/#howToUse).

## Who It Is For

- Those looking for affordable coworking in Kobe
- People who want to drop in around Motomachi or Sannomiya
- Those who need more reliable power and Wi-Fi than a café
- People who prefer not to sign up for membership or monthly plans
- Anyone who may use the space early, late, or overnight

You are welcome to use the space for work, study, or a short break.

## Opening Schedule

We plan to open in late October. No reservation is required. For press inquiries and other questions, please [contact us](/en/contact/).
