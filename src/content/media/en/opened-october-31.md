**ONE COIN COWORKING** has been **open for business since October 31, 2026**.

We aim to be a casual coworking space that feels like working from a café.

Please use the space at your own pace—for work, study, or a break.

## Essentials

**¥500 entry, unlimited time.** No membership, open 24 hours. Pay at the tablet by the entrance, then take any open seat ([See details here](/en/#howToUse)).

Conversation and drinks welcome; Wi-Fi and power outlets included.
