Interior construction at **ONE COIN COWORKING**, a 7-minute walk from [Motomachi Station](/en/#information) in Kobe, continues to move forward.

About a month after we [started interior work](/en/column/interior-construction-started/), wall coverings and ceiling finishes are in place. The space is much closer to how it will look when we open.

## Current progress

![Coworking space with wallpaper, wood-grain ceiling, and flooring work underway](/column/interior-construction-progress/overview.jpg)

Calm grey wallpaper covers the main walls, with accent color in the back area. The ceiling has a wood-grain finish, and flooring is going in. Ceiling equipment such as air conditioning is also being installed.

## Then and now

When work first began, the ceiling was still board substrate and the floor was exposed subfloor.

![Early interior work with ceiling boards and subfloor](/column/interior-construction-started/overview.jpg)

*When construction started ([Interior construction has started](/en/column/interior-construction-started/)). Compare with the photo at the top of this article for today’s progress.*

From framing and wiring through to walls, ceiling, and floor, fit-out has progressed steadily across the space.

## What comes next

We will install lighting and furniture, then carry out final cleaning and inspections ahead of opening.

For more about our opening plans, see our [opening announcement](/en/column/motomachi-kakuyasu-coworking/). For media inquiries and other questions, please use our [contact form](/en/contact/).
