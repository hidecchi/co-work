Many people search for cafés in **Sannomiya** or **Motomachi** where they can open a laptop and get some work done. When a place offers Wi-Fi and outlets, it can feel like an easy option. In practice, **how long you can stay** and **whether it is OK to hold a seat for hours** varies from shop to shop.

In this article we share **what to keep in mind when working from a café** and **another option when you want to focus for several hours or more**.

## 1. Tips for working from a café

Cafés are primarily for food and drink, but they are also used for study and remote work. Keeping the following in mind helps you avoid friction with staff and other guests.

- During **peak hours**, seats fill up quickly; staying for a long time in one spot can inconvenience others.
- Seats with power outlets are sometimes **stools or chairs without a backrest**, which may not suit extended work sessions.

On the other hand, with a good drink in hand, a **short work session** can be very comfortable.

## 2. When you need longer focus: choose a coworking space

If you want to **work with focus for more than a few hours**, a **coworking space** matched to that purpose is worth considering.
